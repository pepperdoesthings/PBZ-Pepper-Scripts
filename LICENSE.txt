Earned Progression
==================

Phantom Blade Zero and all of its content are the property of S-GAME. These are unofficial
fan-made mods with no affiliation to S-GAME. They never modify the game's files: they add
script files that Mods Menu starts. Remove the files to undo them.

Permission
----------
All code in this project (Earned Progression and the build tools in tools\) is original work.
You may use, copy, modify and share it, in whole or in part, for any purpose. Credit is
appreciated but not required.

Game files
----------
No game file, script or asset is included in this project or its releases.

Third-party software
--------------------
Earned Progression needs Mods Menu by SLEEP
(https://www.nexusmods.com/phantombladezero/mods/149). It is a separate download under its own
licence, which does not allow redistribution, and none of its files are included here.

tools\get-tools.bat downloads repak by trumank (https://github.com/trumank/repak). It is not part
of this project and keeps its own licence, which is included in its download.

References
---------
For the creation of this mod, PBZ Phantom Souls by PVLW (https://www.nexusmods.com/phantombladezero/mods/36) was used as a
reference to understand how the game can be modded and specific parts of the Earned Progression mod - particularly the respawn dust percentage and safe radius for bells - were heavily inspired by their work. None of its files are included in this project, but thanks is nonetheless extended to the developer.
