ScriptModLoader and Earned Progression
=======================================

Phantom Blade Zero and all of its content are the property of S-GAME. These are unofficial
fan-made mods with no affiliation to S-GAME. They never modify the game's files: they add
override paks that the engine mounts on top of the originals. Remove the files to undo them.

Game files
----------
ScriptModLoader replaces one game script, ProjectPBZ/Content/JavaScript/UI/Widget/Foundation/
WeaponNormalTextEntry.js, with its own small file. The game's version of that file is an empty
stub; the replacement keeps the same empty stub and adds one call that starts the loader.

No other game file, script or asset is included in the paks or in this project.

Third-party tools
-----------------
tools\get-tools.bat downloads repak and retoc by trumank (https://github.com/trumank/repak,
https://github.com/trumank/retoc). They are not part of this project and keep their own
licences, which are included in their downloads.

References
---------
For the creation of this mod, PBZ Phantom Souls by PVLW (https://www.nexusmods.com/phantombladezero/mods/36) was used as a
reference to understand how the game can be modded and specific parts of the Earned Progression mod - particularly the respawn dust percentage and safe radius for bells - were heavily inspired by their work. None of its files are included in this project, but thanks is nonetheless extended to the developer.
